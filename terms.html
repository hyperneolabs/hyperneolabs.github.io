The service

HyperNeo Review is an AI code-review service. For the GitHub repositories you enroll, it reviews pull requests and posts findings as inline review comments (severity and confidence) plus a one-line verdict. You bring your own provider API keys (BYOK); inference for your reviews runs against the provider account you configure, on your key.

Reviews are advisory by default: they inform, they do not decide. The humans who merge remain responsible for every line of code and every merge decision.

What we ask of you

  • Keys you add must be yours to use; your use of the underlying providers is subject to their terms as well as these.
  • Don't use the service to abuse GitHub, your providers, or us.

Plans and payment

  • Free — public repositories, and a 14-day no-card trial on private repositories.
  • Pro — $15 per developer per month, or $12 per developer per month billed annually.
  • Design Partner — $7.50 per developer per month, lifetime, locked at enrollment for early users.

There is no usage metering. Because inference runs on your keys, we bill per developer — not per review, not per token. During the soft open no card is required: design partners are invoiced manually and billing terms are agreed by email; card payments arrive with checkout.

You can stop any time — uninstall the GitHub App and delete your keys. Service ends with the current billing period; nothing renews silently.
